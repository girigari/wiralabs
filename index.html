The problem

Last-mile mountain regions are drowning in waste that no model can reach.

Every circularity model assumes urban infrastructure — collection systems, processing facilities, formal supply chains. In last-mile Himalayan regions, none of that exists. Construction debris and textile waste accumulate in riverbeds and village commons. The communities bear the cost.

WIRA Labs builds the missing infrastructure from the ground up — community-sourced supply chains, local processing, and products designed for the mountain construction market. Wealth from waste. Made here. For here.

How it works

01

Community collection

Village-level supply chains source C&D glass and fabric waste — creating income at the point of collection.

02

Local processing

Energy-efficient methods adapted to mountain contexts. No heavy machinery. No urban dependency.

03

Product making

Waste glass becomes tiles. Waste fabric becomes panels. Refuse becomes resource.

04

Mountain market

Affordable locally made products sold to the mountain construction industry — closing the circle.
